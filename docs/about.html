Privacy & processing

Photographs, masks and exports stay in this browser. There is no photo-upload endpoint, account, analytics, paid API or local-server dependency. Reloading closes the session; download your finished image before leaving.

Automatic masking is optional. Only when requested, this editor loads the pinned IMG.LY background-removal 1.5.5 code from jsDelivr and model/runtime assets from staticimgly.com. These services receive normal asset requests (including your IP address), not your photographs. Browser caching may reduce subsequent downloads, but offline inference is not guaranteed. Manual correction and imported masks do not load a model.

Original detail, honest limits

Original photographs provide all final RGB pixels. Segmentation receives a separate lossless PNG, with its longest side limited to 1600 pixels. ISNet internally uses its own model resolution. Alpha is edited as Float32 at up to 2048 pixels on the longest side and converted to Canvas alpha only for composition. Larger export dimensions do not invent more detail.

The browser normalizes EXIF orientation and performs color conversion. Canvas output is an sRGB-oriented raster workflow, not an ICC-preserving print workflow. Partly transparent source edges can contain original background color; imported masks do not reconstruct uncontaminated hair RGB. Inspect edges before publishing.

Foreground selection is not infallible. Use a Subject area to constrain inference; Keep person removes disconnected components but can also remove detached fingers or hands. Add and Erase, with one-operation Undo, are the reliable fallback. The editor does not claim that combining models improves segmentation.

Files are limited to 25 MB and 16 megapixels before decoding. Both effects default off. Use Compact mode on lower-memory devices; a browser cannot enforce a universal RAM limit. A cancelled worker is terminated. No model is preloaded.
